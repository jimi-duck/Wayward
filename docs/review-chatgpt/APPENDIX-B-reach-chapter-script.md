# The Reach: Chapter Script

2026-10-25 · @Jimi Duck

The Reach is the first leg of the Warden's Road. Juno arrives with an unsigned message and a place name. She leaves with her grandmother's name, a signed order, a ledger, a hunter on the line, and the first proof that the truth gives her away. It runs eight stops and about forty minutes. This is a draft of every line the player sees, in play order, to read through the level as a whole. English only for now; each line needs a German twin before it ships.

**How the copy is marked.** *Card* pauses the world and needs an answer. *Radio* is a subtitle on the radio strip. *Notice* is the quiet low-centre line. *Objective* sits top left. *Job* is a job offer card. **Existing** marks copy already in the game, kept word for word. Everything else is new and provisional.

## When lines play

The When column names a trigger, not a stopwatch. Every line plays at the first safe moment after its trigger, and these rules decide what safe means. They matter most for the ghost at Hollis and the five steps at the Narrows, where a beat played over a fight would be lost.

- **Story beats queue, they never drop.** Every card, radio line and Wall entry in this script waits its turn. Nothing is skipped because the player was fighting, docked or jumping out. A beat still waiting at a jump carries over and plays at the next stop's first free moment.

- **A free moment** means no hostile inside weapons range, no incoming fire for 4 s, no card open, no job fight running, and not inside a gate ring or a jump. Seconds in the When column are the least wait after a free moment, never a fixed time.

- **One voice at a time.** Radio lines play one after another, never on top of each other, with at least 2 s between them. A line in a sequence waits for the line before it.

- **The flight path stays clear.** Radio sits on the radio strip. It never covers the centre, the objective, hit and damage feedback, or combat alerts. If a fight starts mid-line, the line finishes on the strip and the next one waits.

- **Cards wait for calm.** A story card pauses the world, so it never opens with a hostile inside weapons range. Job calls keep their existing rule: 3 to 4 s after arrival, never over training or a fight.

- **Clues live in the Wall.** Anything the player needs later is written to the Wall the moment it is earned, even if its radio line has not played yet: the order and its signature, Ada's name, the ledger initials, the ghost, Ada's note. Radio adds a voice; the Wall is the record.

- **The ghost waits too.** It never appears during a fight. If one starts, it holds off and Sam's lines wait with it. If the player jumps first, the Wall still logs the contact.

- **The Narrows plays in order.** Each of its five steps waits for the one before. If the blockade fight is running, the mast still lights, but Ada's note waits until the pickets break off or Juno is clear, and the Patience never arrives before the note has closed.

- **Pursuit is always counted in days.** The Course screen and every notice give the hunt as days behind, to the half day. The leg starts at 2 days. An amber notice fires when it closes to 1 day, a red one at half a day, and at zero the pursuit arrives in the system (the existing Custody pickets). Vesta shows no pursuit and the gap does not close while Juno rests there.

## Stop 1 · Northfold (Landfall)

The leg opens quietly. The Course screen first appears as the Wayward flies through the Jump Gate out of the first system past Cerro (the audit dock system), **before** Northfold: that jump is the one that reaches it. Sam's second line matters. It tells the player their one friend has a range, and that range ends at the Narrows. The leg now has a clock that is not a number.

**The order**, as the document shows it. The wording is what the player reads; the signature is what the Wall records.

> **CONTINUANCE EMERGENCY AUTHORITY · ORDER OF CONTINUITY 1** Effective at once. The outer relays at the Narrows and beyond are to be powered down. Wardens on watch will surrender their keys to the attending officer. The closure is to be recorded as a failure from neglect. **Authorised:** L. C.

**If Juno cuts Ilse off,** she does not lose the order. It only arrives later and from a distance. At Vesta (stop 5) a recorded message from Ilse carries the same document and names Ada, so every player reaches the Narrows holding the order and knowing whose routine it is. What cutting her off costs is Ilse herself: no second voice, no thanks, and no epilogue line for her.

Juno has always carried the Aster name. What she never had was the story behind it: her family were told Ada was lost in service, and nothing more. The hint on her answer says so, which keeps the scene a revelation rather than a contradiction.

### The Wall entry for the ghost

Opened with W, in the Wall's existing format.

> **Unknown contact · Hollis** *What it proves:* something held at the edge of your scope and matched your speed, and it came closer as the tank filled. *What it does not prove:* what it was, or what it wanted. *Next lead:* none yet.

The Wall says *none yet* on purpose. It is the only entry in the leg with no lead, and that absence is the hook.

**What the handshake gives away.** Calloway has been hunting the Wayward since the Cerro breach. That is the existing pursuit: the Custody pickets and the days on the Course screen are hers, even though the Reach only ever calls it the Custody. Until tonight she has been chasing a stolen hauler and guessing at its lane. The relay changes two things at once. It names Juno: the Warden channel announces Ada's key and the hull that answered it, so the Offices learn that the stolen ship carries Ada Aster's archive. And it gives away the road: a lit Warden relay is a point on the old Warden route, so the Offices can see which relays Juno must reach next. From here Calloway knows what Juno found and where she has to go.

In play this is one change to the hunt. It stops losing time to guessing which lane she took (the same rule the hunt already uses when a pilot flies straight), and the Course screen's pursuit line takes her name and adds *knows your route*. The Patience was already holding beyond the blockade, as the Offices hold every gate out of the Reach; the broadcast tells her which hauler to turn for. This is the first proof of the leg's engine: the truth is a beacon.

>
> The Narrows was mine the night they closed it.
>
> I was told to switch it off and forget the key. I switched it off. I did not forget.
>
