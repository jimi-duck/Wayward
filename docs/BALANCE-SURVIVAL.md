# Survival modes balance report

Generated by `npm run pilot:survival`: the scripted pilot flew the arcade
(6 seeds × 2 strategies, 10:00 each: random, torpedo+defence) and
2 whole journeys in each mode, on the same seeds. Prospector is the
baseline; Drifter and Raider are read against it. The pilot flies one way in
every mode: it shoots what is in front of it, takes what lies near, mines when
the tank is low. It does not wait out a skirmish or choose its fights, so a
mode's salvage loop is a floor here, not what a player who plays it can do.

## The Arcade sky, calibrated for the 30-Krail bay

With every Arcade bay pick at 30 Krail the baseline pilot (120 runs, 15:00 cap,
ten build strategies) got too easy. Jimi asked for harder. Prospector's `sky` row
now carries the answer, and Drifter and Raider are scaled by the same factors, so
the three keep their distance. The Story reads none of it (its elite chance moved
to `pursuit.elite`, still 1 for Prospector).

On main with the hull, pacing and bay changes of 7 October:

| Prospector sky (traffic, toughness, fire, elites, hazard) | Mean time | Modules a run | At the 15:00 cap |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1, 1, 1, 1, 1 (before) | 12:12 | 26.7 | 55 / 120 |
| 1.8, 1.8, 1.3, 1.8, 1.5 | 11:27 | 24.7 | 40 / 120 |
| **2.2, 2.2, 1.4, 2, 1.6 (now)** | **10:15** | **21.7** | **19 / 120** |

Aim: 20 to 25 of 120 at the cap. Modules a run fall less than hoped (the aim was
about 15): a harder sky ends runs sooner but does not slow the Krail. Cutting
Krail income would, but it also thins the Story's economy, so it is left for a
human playtest to ask for. A 20% rise barely moves anything; the pilot reads
toughness and crowd size most.

Drifter's sky is now 1.55 / 1.85 / 1.75 / 1 / 0.8, Raider's 2.75 / 2.4 / 1.25 / 2.8 / 2.1.
In the small three-mode run above (6 seeds, 10:00 cap) Drifter survives 83%,
Prospector 17% and Raider 50%: Raider's richer wrecks and repairs keep it ahead
of Prospector on survival, as in the first report. Raider's crowd (2.75 times the
old hulls) is the number most likely to feel too much to a person.

## Reading it

- **Three different games.** Drifter has the least sky and the longest calm. Raider has the most time in combat and gets about 85% of its arcade Krail from wrecks, against 46% for Prospector.
- **Raider is not poorer, it is different.** Its Krail total is above Prospector's but 85% of it comes from killing.
- **Nobody is stuck**, and every Story journey got through, in every mode.
- **The pilot does not wait out skirmishes**, so the Raider's salvage loop here is a floor. The skirmish is covered by `tests/survival.test.ts`.
- The numbers are first guesses: no human has played the three modes on the harder sky yet.
