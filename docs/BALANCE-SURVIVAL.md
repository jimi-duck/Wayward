# Survival modes balance report

Generated by `npm run pilot:survival`: the scripted pilot flew the arcade
(6 seeds × 2 strategies, 10:00 each: random, torpedo+defence) and
2 whole journeys in each mode, on the same seeds. Prospector is the
baseline; Drifter and Raider are read against it. The pilot flies one way in
every mode: it shoots what is in front of it, takes what lies near, mines when
the tank is low. It does not wait out a skirmish or choose its fights, so a
mode's salvage loop is a floor here, not what a player who plays it can do.

## The Arcade sky and income, calibrated for the 30-Krail bay

With every Arcade bay pick at 30 Krail the baseline pilot (120 runs, 15:00 cap, ten
build strategies) got too easy, so Prospector's `sky` row carries the answer and
Drifter and Raider are scaled by the same factors. Two parts: the hulls (traffic,
toughness, fire, elites, hazard) and `income`, a multiplier on the Krail the Arcade's
rock and wrecks drop. Only the Arcade reads the sky group, so the Story is unchanged.

| Prospector | Mean time | Modules a run | At the 15:00 cap |
|---|---|---|---|
| no change (bay at 30) | 12:12 | 26.7 | 55 / 120 |
| sky 2.2 / 2.2 / 1.4 / 2 / 1.6, income 1 | 10:15 | 21.7 | 19 / 120 |
| sky 2 / 2 / 1.35 / 1.9 / 1.5, income 0.8 | 10:26 | 19.1 | 26 / 120 |
| **same sky, income 0.75 (now), with destructible bulwarks on main** | **9:37** | **16.2** | **14 / 120** |
| same sky, income 0.6 | 9:28 | 13.7 | 14 / 120 |
| same sky, income 0.5 | 9:05 | 11.1 | 6 / 120 |

The aim was about 15 modules and 20 to 25 of 120 at the cap; the two pull apart
(less Krail also ends runs sooner), so modules won and the cap landed a little low.
A 20% rise in the hulls alone barely moves the pilot. Income 0.75 is the line to
move if a person finds Arcade too thin or too easy. Drifter's income is 0.9,
Raider's 0.75 (its Krail is mostly from wrecks, so the cut bites there too).

In the three-mode run above (6 seeds, 10:00 cap) Drifter survives 92%, Prospector
50% and Raider 58%: Raider's richer wrecks and repairs keep it level with
Prospector on survival, and it scores most. Raider's crowd (2.5 times the old
hulls) is the number most likely to feel too much to a person.

The pilot does not wait out skirmishes, so Raider's salvage here is a floor. All of
these numbers are first guesses: no human has played the three modes on the harder
sky yet.
