# Survival modes balance report

Generated by `npm run pilot:survival`: the scripted pilot flew the arcade
(6 seeds × 2 strategies, 10:00 each: random, torpedo+defence) and
4 whole journeys in each mode, on the same seeds. Prospector is the
baseline; Drifter and Raider are read against it. The pilot flies one way in
every mode: it shoots what is in front of it, takes what lies near, mines when
the tank is low. It does not wait out a skirmish or choose its fights, so a
mode's salvage loop is a floor here, not what a player who plays it can do.

## Reading it

- **Three different games.** Drifter has the least sky (1.95 contacts a minute in the Story against 2.46 and 2.81), the longest calm and the richest tank at the chart. Raider has the most sky and the most time in combat (48% in the arcade) and gets 83% of its arcade Krail and 86% of its Story Cyrite from wrecks, against 43% and 20% for Prospector.
- **Raider is not poorer, it is different.** Its arcade Krail total (144 a minute) is level with Prospector's (131); the mix flips. Its Story Cyrite from all sources (14 a minute) is above Prospector's (5.9), but it all comes from killing.
- **Raider survives least in the arcade** (67% to 75% to 83%) and scores most: a harder sky for a better score, as intended. The gap between modes is inside the noise of twelve runs; a human playtest should settle it.
- **Nobody is stuck**, and every Story journey got through, in every mode.
- **The pilot does not wait out skirmishes**, so the Raider's salvage loop here is a floor. The skirmish itself is covered by `tests/survival.test.ts`.
- The numbers are first guesses: no human has played the three modes yet.
