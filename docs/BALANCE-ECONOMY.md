## What it says

- **No snowball.** A pilot that pours its Cyrite into the utility branch earns *less* than one that builds guns: 23 against 39
  Cyrite a minute at systems 61 to 70, and 91 against 111 a sortie. Combat-first income climbs with depth, because wrecks pay
  more as hulls toughen; economy-first stays flat from system 40, since its extra 4 levels of tractor, matrix and prospector
  add far less than the kills it gives up (7.9 fight levels against 13.7 by the end).
- **Saving takes about one sortie** for either build (0.8 to 1.6 sorties of income for the cheapest tier-2 level), so there
  is always something to buy, as the economy asked for.
- **Danger is similar.** Plates lost a sortie and wrecked shares sit in the same range (combat 0.4 and 8%, economy 0.7 and
  0% at the end); four journeys a side cannot separate them.
- **Utility is the weak branch, not the strong one.** Matrix (+12% a level) and Prospector (+50% crystal chance) multiply
  rock income, but rock is a small share of what a sortie earns late. If anything, the branch is worth a look for being
  too thin to be a real choice, not for being too good.

## Caveats

Four journeys a side, a scripted pilot, the first 70 systems only. The pilot does not take jobs, whose pay is on top of
this. Economy-first journeys flew 60 systems on average against 70 (some stopped before the cap; the report does not say
why). Nothing was nerfed or buffed; this is a measurement. Re-run with `UPG_STRAT=economy|combat` and `UPG_ECONOMY=dir`
(see the head of `tools/upgrades.pilot.ts`).
