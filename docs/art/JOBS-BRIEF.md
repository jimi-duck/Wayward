# Wayward artwork: the job pictures, redrawn tall (all in one)

*For someone who has never seen this project: for ChatGPT, Lovable or any image tool. Attach the ZIP `wayward-job-pictures-current.zip` and paste this whole file. Everything you need is here.*

## What this is
**WAYWARD** is a 3D space-survival game for desktop and phone browsers. You fly a worn yellow-orange ore hauler, the *Wayward*, through a dark industrial frontier. The mood is cyberpunk frontier, "used, not dirty": old, patched, regulated technology.

The game offers side jobs in a pop-up card: the words on the left, **one tall picture down the right side of the card**, as tall as the words, inside the card's margins and in a thin pale border. The 25 pictures in the ZIP are **wide (16:9, 1280×720)**. The card now needs them **tall**, so each one has to be recomposed, not just cropped.

## The task
Take each picture in the ZIP and make a **tall portrait version**.

- **Size: 2:3 portrait, 1280×1920 pixels or larger** (the game resizes it to 960×1440). The card shows it about 325 to 350 px wide and 475 to 530 px tall, so it must read at a glance.
- **Same picture, new shape.** Keep the scene, the ships, the palette, the lighting and the level of detail of the attached picture. Do not invent a different scene. Extend the sky, rock, haze and structure upward and downward to fill the tall frame, and move or stack the main elements so everything important sits in the **middle 60% of the width** and the **middle 80% of the height**. The card crops the sides a little on some screens, and its right-hand corners are cut, so keep detail away from the edges and corners.
- The **Wayward** (a long, low, worn yellow-orange ore hauler with split side engine pods) stays in every picture it is in now, small in the frame, clearly readable.
- Where the old picture spreads things out sideways (ships in a row, a wide station), **stack them vertically** instead: the main subject in the upper middle, the Wayward in the lower middle, calm dark space or rock between and around.
- No text, lettering, numbers, logos, UI, frames or borders inside any picture. No lens flare, no neon pink, no cartoon look.

## The look (match the attached pictures)
Style: cinematic, richly detailed sci-fi concept art in the look of the Wayward title art (the attached pictures): dark space, painterly-realistic, high detail, volumetric haze. Industrial cyberpunk frontier, "used, not dirty": advanced technology that is old, patched, regulated and repurposed; everything has a previous owner, a serial number and a warning label. Worn painted metal, faded paint, replacement panels, exposed fasteners, cable runs, stencilled markings, maintenance labels; lava-veined dark rock where rock appears; warm amber window lights against deep blue-black shadow. Palette: mostly dark and desaturated (void black #080D0E, industrial off-white #D8D5C6, dust grey #596160, deep rust brown #4A3A27) with the Wayward's yellow-orange (#D99A27) as the warm anchor. Accents sparingly: signal teal #39AEB7 only for screens and information; alert red #C94A3F only for threat; a dull gold #C9AB66 only for caution. Nebula purple only far in the background. No neon pink. Lighting: low-key, one clear key light with soft rim light and haze, glow kept restrained and small (engine blue, window orange); nothing blown out.

## What to deliver
- Every picture a **separate PNG**, named exactly as its `Save as` line (`jobs__sc_toll.png`). **One picture per image, never a contact sheet.**
- One ZIP of all 25. If you cannot make a ZIP, one PDF with one picture per page and the name in a caption outside the picture.
- If you run out of room, stop after a picture and carry on when told "continue". Do the pictures in the order below.
- Do not change anything else.

## If it goes wrong
- **A sheet when a picture was asked:** reply "make this as one single full-frame picture, not a sheet".
- **Text or a border in a picture:** reply "remove all text, lines and borders".
- **The scene changed too much:** reply "keep the original picture's scene and ships; only extend it and rearrange it into a tall frame", and attach the original again.
- **Too small or soft:** reply "same picture, larger and much sharper".
