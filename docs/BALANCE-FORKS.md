# Balance: forks

Seeds per set: 60. A fork is a system with two or more lanes on. "Thin": the lanes differ on at most one thing a player can weigh. "No cost or opportunity apart": same style, port, breather, place, days and jump fluid within 3 (only the kind and the gate distance differ).

## three regions

- forks 1145, thin 43 (3.8%), no cost or opportunity apart 49 (4.3%)
- lanes per fork: 2: 510, 3: 635
- forks where the lanes differ in each: kind 97%, style 84%, port 51%, days 70%, gate 75%, fluid 35%
