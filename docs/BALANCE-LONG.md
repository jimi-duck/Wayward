# Journey balance report

Generated by `npm run pilot:journey`: the scripted pilot flew 24
whole journeys (4 seeds per strategy and policy), each a fresh chart.
It flies every system as it flies the Breakout (mines, docks once when
the stores are low or the hull wants patching, then
tops the tank up at the port if it is short, makes for the Jump Gate once the tank holds the jump), and plots every
jump with a cautious route planner: toward the Threshold, away from the Net
and from where Calloway was last seen, toward a port when supplies are short and
toward Cyrite when the tank is. It answers every comms card by one policy.
