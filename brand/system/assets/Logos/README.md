# Logos

Drawn files for use outside the system's own pages. In this system's previews and in the game, use the inline SVG (`ab-mk`, `ab-wm`), which takes its colours from the theme.

- `asteroid-blaster-mark.svg`: the mark, Breach, in `signal` #00F0FF and `dilithium` #C77DFF. For dark grounds.
- `asteroid-blaster-mark-ink.svg`: the mark in one ink, #E8F3F7. For dark grounds and photography.
- `asteroid-blaster-mark-paper.svg`: the mark in one ink, #0A141C. For light grounds and print.
- `asteroid-blaster-wordmark.svg`: the one-line wordmark, ASTEROID in #E8F3F7, BLASTER in #00F0FF. For dark grounds. Minimum 160px wide.
- `asteroid-blaster-wordmark-paper.svg`: the one-line wordmark in #0A141C and #00707D. For light grounds.
- `asteroid-blaster-stacked.svg`: the stacked wordmark for dark grounds. Minimum 96px wide.
- `asteroid-blaster-stacked-paper.svg`: the stacked wordmark for light grounds.
- `asteroid-blaster-app-icon.svg`: the app and favicon tile: the mark at 70% on a `void` tile cut at the corners, with a faint `signal` hairline.
